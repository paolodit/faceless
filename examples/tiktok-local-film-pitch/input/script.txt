Our seagulls audit your chips.

They do not steal them.

Every Friday, when the rain starts sideways and the bins begin their little dance down the pavement, the gull council gathers on the roof of the arcade.

Big Glyn takes the minutes. Sandra watches the promenade. Tiny Kev controls the car park.

Then a tourist drops a tray of chips and the entire council holds an emergency vote.

No one knows the rules.

Everyone knows the gulls are in charge.
