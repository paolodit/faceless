I call it the dopamine tax.

It is the tiny emotional fee you pay every time you open an app to do one useful thing.

You check one message.

Then you see a notification.

Then you remember someone else is already better at the thing you are trying to learn.

Ten minutes later, you are not relaxed, not informed, and somehow worried about becoming a person who owns a standing desk.

The trick is not to become perfectly disciplined.

The trick is to notice the toll booth before you drive through it.
