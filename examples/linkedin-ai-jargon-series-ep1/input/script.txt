AI jargon can make simple work feel more complicated than it is.

Here is episode one: the plain-English version of the words you keep hearing.

A prompt is the instruction you give the AI. Specific prompts beat clever prompts.

Context is the information the AI can see. No context usually means generic output.

Memory is what carries across sessions. Useful memory stays relevant and easy to correct.

RAG means retrieval augmented generation. In plain English, search your knowledge before answering.

An agent is AI that can take steps toward a goal. It still needs boundaries.

A loop is the repeated plan, act, check cycle. Great systems know when to stop.

Guardrails are the rules that keep output inside bounds. Constraints beat hope.

Evals are tests for output quality. Measure before you scale.

If a tool cannot explain these basics clearly, pause before you buy it.
